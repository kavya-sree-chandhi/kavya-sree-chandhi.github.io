About Me

I am an AI & Data Analytics Specialist with over 5+ years of experience architecting end-to-end data processing pipelines, interactive Business Intelligence (BI) dashboards, and production-grade artificial intelligence automation systems. Grounded in core computer science principles—including data structures, algorithms, object-oriented programming, and system design—I specialize in converting complex, unstructured, or high-velocity datasets into actionable business intelligence and operational strategies. A primary cornerstone of my career resides in the Banking and Financial Services (BFSI) sector, where I have built extensive expertise in transaction risk analytics, anomaly detection, automated ETL workflows, and regulatory compliance frameworks such as KYC (Know Your Customer) and AML (Anti-Money Laundering). Across my career, I have delivered scalable solutions across diverse domains: as a Generative AI Engineer at UWorld, I architected vector search pipelines using ChromaDB, automated content review with Azure OpenAI and LangGraph, and monitored platform performance via Azure Application Insights; as an Associate Professional Software Engineer at DXC Technology, I designed interactive Tableau and Grafana dashboards, optimized complex SQL queries, and administered access controls for over 5,000 enterprise users under strict ISO/IEC security standards; and as a Data Analyst at Pelican IT Solutions, I developed machine learning models (Random Forest, Isolation Forest, Autoencoders) that boosted transaction fraud detection accuracy by 20% while reducing manual ETL processing efforts by 30% using Python and SQL.

Complementing my industry achievements, I hold a Master of Science in Computer Science with a 3.916 / 4.0 GPA from the University of North Texas, Denton, with advanced coursework in Machine Learning, AI, Big Data Analytics, Cloud Computing, and Software Architecture. My academic research spans building an AI Research Agent for Healthcare Diagnostics using Python, Streamlit, LangChain, and LLaMA3 with automated citation validation; an Automatic Attendance Monitoring & Analytics System using OpenCV and Django; a Blockchain and AI Data Security Architecture for patient-controlled medical records; and statistical modeling on survey records of over 8,900 individuals to analyze youth behavioral health trends. Beyond technical engineering, I am deeply committed to cross-functional leadership and social responsibility; as a Volunteer Software Engineer at the Network Theory Applied Research Institute (NTARI), I enhanced modular frontend interfaces and analyzed backend data structures within an Agile team, while as Associate President on the Executive Board of Street Cause SWEC during the COVID-19 pandemic, I led relief efforts including the Slum Adoption Project, delivering monthly essential supplies to 262+ families across 10+ slums. Guided by an engineering philosophy centered on scalability, data integrity, and user impact, I aim to continually build secure, high-performing AI and analytics solutions that empower organizations to make confident, data-driven decisions.
